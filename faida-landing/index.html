Questions people ask

Do I need to download an app?

No. If you have WhatsApp, you have everything you need.

Does it cost anything to find out what I qualify for?

No. Finding out what fits you is free.

Can I write in Kiswahili?

Yes. Faida speaks English and Kiswahili.

Will Faida do things without asking me?

No. It asks you before anything is sent. You can say STOP at any time.

What if a service can't be done online?

Faida tells you exactly what to bring and where to go, and shows you the nearest office.

Is my information safe?

Your details are kept private and used only for your own applications. You can ask us to delete them.

I run a SACCO, county or NGO. How do we start?

Talk to us. We will list your programme and show you who it would reach.
