Who it's forAnyone offering something to people

Your people, whatever you offer.

  • Counties, wards and constituencies

    Bursaries, county funds, permits and public participation. Notices go out as PDFs and on notice boards, and forms wait at ward offices. Faida tells residents who qualify and have chosen to hear, fills in the form with them, and brings people who usually miss the budget forum into it.

  • National programmes and agencies

    Registration windows of a few days, places capped per ward, USSD-only applications. Faida tells eligible people the day a window opens, walks them through the steps, and keeps trainees attending.

  • NGOs, community and faith groups, foundations and development partners

    Reach eligible people outside your usual network, run intake, cases and feedback in one place, and send funders reach and outcome data from the records that run the programme.

  • SACCOs, co-operatives, banks and insurers

    Many members miss circulars. Faida tells each member about the dividend, loan, bonus or cover that fits them, under your name, and handles renewals.

  • Employers, colleges and training providers

    Fake adverts use your name, and real ones draw floods of unqualified applicants. Faida sends openings to people who meet your requirements, checks adverts people forward to it, and handles onboarding and attendance.

  • Health, agriculture, housing and utilities

    Screenings, farmer registration, seasonal inputs, connections and housing rounds. Faida tells people when and where, what to bring, and reminds them the day before.

  • 1. List it once.

    Who qualifies, what you need, how many places, and when it closes. Through a form, on WhatsApp, or by connecting your system. Faida checks it and shows you who it would reach before it goes out.

  • 2. The right people hear first.

    Only people who meet your rules and chose to hear. On WhatsApp, by SMS and USSD for basic phones, and with a paper form or an office visit where your programme needs one. Faida answers their questions so your staff don't have to.

  • 3. Applications arrive complete.

    Checked against your rules, with documents attached, then sent to you or entered straight into your system. Your offer reaches the people it was meant for, not the well connected.

Run the programmeFrom enrolment to closure

Run the whole programme in the conversation.

Programmes lose people between selection and the last session: a missed message, a forgotten training day, a document still outstanding. Faida keeps each participant on track and your team on top of every case.

  • Set up

    Target groups, eligibility rules, locations, funding limits, timelines and required documents, set once.

  • Intake and selection

    Registration, consent, household details and documents, collected in conversation. Rules applied, gaps flagged, and a selection you can explain.

  • Enrol and deliver

    Selected applicants told, next steps explained, acceptance collected. Appointments, training, distributions and referrals coordinated, with reminders.

  • Follow up and close

    Every case tracked to resolution. Attendance, completion and outcomes recorded. Participants told when the programme closes, and final feedback collected.

Feedback and accountabilityIn their own words

Ask how it went, the way people talk.

After a training day or a distribution, Faida asks how it went. People answer in their own words or by voice note. A concern becomes a case with a reference number and goes to your team. Your team responds, Faida tells the participant, and the case stays open until it's closed.

Learn and reportFor programme teams and their funders

See where delivery is failing.

Faida counts applications, and it also shows you where things break. It answers questions like these:

  • Which eligible communities aren't being reached?
  • Where do applicants give up?
  • Which document or requirement causes the most friction?
  • Are selected participants getting the service they were promised?
  • Which concerns are still open?
  • Which interventions lead to better outcomes?

Programme dashboards, reach totals by ward, gender, age and disability, and funder reports, from the same records that run the programme.

Fair by designFor the people you serve

An opportunity, never an advert.

  • People choose to hear from you, and can stop at any time.
  • Every offer is labelled with who is offering it.
  • Offers are ranked by what they give the person. Nobody can pay to skip your rules.
  • Every applicant is checked against your rules before reaching you.
  • You see what people send you when they apply. Reports show totals, never names.
  • Faida never asks anyone for their M-Pesa or bank PIN.

Questions organisations ask

Questions organisations ask

Will Faida flood us with applications?

No. Only people who meet your rules are told, and each application is checked before it reaches you.

How many people in our area can Faida reach?

It depends on how many people in your area use Faida and have chosen to hear about programmes like yours. Before your programme goes out, Faida shows you how many people it would reach, so you can plan other outreach for everyone else.

How do you know applicants are genuine?

Faida checks each applicant against your rules and the documents they share, and flags anything that doesn't match. Your team makes the final decision.

Do participants need a smartphone or an app?

No app to download. People with smartphones use WhatsApp. People with basic phones get SMS and can use USSD. Field officers reach people who are offline.

Does it work with our existing systems?

Yes. List through a form or on WhatsApp, or connect your system so applications go straight in. Reports can be exported.

How is participants' data used?

You receive the applications people send you, with what your programme asks for. Reports show totals by ward, gender, age and disability, never named individuals. Participants give consent, are told who can see their answers, and can ask for their details to be deleted.

Is Faida an advertising network?

No. Every offer has to give people something on clear terms: money, a service, a place, a job or a right. Commercial products come only from regulated providers, are always labelled, and are never ranked by payment.

What does it cost?

It depends on the size of the programme and which parts you use. Tell us about yours and we'll send a quote.

Can we start small?

Yes. Start with one programme, or one ward, and add more when it works.
