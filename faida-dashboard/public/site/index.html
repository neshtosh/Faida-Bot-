Questions people ask

Questions people ask

Do I need to download an app?

No. If you have WhatsApp, you have everything you need. Even without a smartphone, you can use SMS or USSD.

How does Faida know what fits me?

You tell it a little about yourself once: where you live, your work, your family. Faida checks everything on offer and every deadline against that, and notices moments that open new doors, like finishing school or turning 70. When something fits, it messages you. You can change or delete those details at any time.

Does it cost anything?

Finding out what fits you is free. Some services, like filing for you, may carry a small fee, always shown before you say YES.

Will Faida do things without asking me?

You decide. By default it asks before anything is sent. You can say STOP at any time.

Someone sent me a job offer. Can Faida check it?

Yes. Forward it to Faida. It checks the offer against the organisation's official channels and tells you plainly whether it is genuine. Government recruitment never asks you to pay.

What if something can't be done online?

Many offers need a paper form, a USSD code or a visit. Faida fills in the form for you to print, walks you through the code step by step, or tells you exactly where to go, when, and what to bring. Then it reminds you the day before.

Is my information safe?

Your details are private and used only for your own applications. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Our organisation has something to offer people. How do we list it?

Whether you are a county, a ward, a SACCO, an NGO, an employer or a college: tell us what you offer, who qualifies and when it closes, through a form or on WhatsApp. Faida verifies it and shows you who it would reach before it goes out.
