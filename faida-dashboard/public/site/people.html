At homeEvery stage of life opens something

Which of these is happening at home?

  • Turning 18

    ID, KRA PIN, voter registration, youth funds and the NYOTA youth programme

  • School results or Grade 10

    Ward and county bursaries, HELB loans, KUCCPS university placement and scholarships

  • Looking for work

    Real jobs at home and abroad, checked for scams, plus training and internships

  • Starting or running a business

    Permit, KRA, youth and women funds, and the 30% of tenders set aside

  • A baby on the way

    SHA maternity cover and a birth certificate

  • Living with a disability

    An NCPWD card (disability registration), which opens a tax exemption and a share of tenders

  • A parent turning 70

    Inua Jamii, a monthly payment

  • Planting season

    Farmer registration for subsidised fertiliser, and crop and livestock insurance

  • A death in the family

    Succession, NSSF survivor benefits, and money left in old accounts

  • Drought or floods

    Relief registration and cash transfers

  • Every June

    Your KRA return

Each one opens something. Most of it closes on a date, and some of it needs a step done first. Faida tells you in time and starts that step.

Questions people ask

Questions people ask

Do I need to download an app?

No. If you have WhatsApp, you have everything you need. Even without a smartphone, you can use SMS or USSD. Whether you message Faida first or an organisation signs you up, all you do is reply.

How does Faida know what fits me?

You tell it a little about yourself once: where you live, your work, your family. Faida checks everything on offer and every deadline against that, and notices moments that open new doors, like finishing school or turning 70. When something fits, it messages you. You can change or delete those details at any time.

Is Faida from the government?

No. Faida is an independent Kenyan service. The rules for each offer come from the organisation offering it, and Faida applies through their official channels.

Faida messaged me, but I never signed up. Why?

An organisation you deal with, such as your county, your SACCO or an NGO, may have added your number for one of its programmes. Faida's first message says who added you. Reply YES to continue, or STOP and you won't hear from Faida again. Faida never asks for money or your M-Pesa PIN.

Does it cost anything?

Finding out what you qualify for is free. If you ask Faida to file for you and there's a fee, you see it in shillings before you say YES. Nobody should charge you to receive a benefit.

How does Faida make money if it's free?

Organisations pay Faida to reach the people their programmes are for and to receive complete applications. Services like filing on your behalf may carry a fee, always shown first.

Will Faida message me all the time?

No. It writes only when something fits you or a date is close. Reply NOT NOW to skip one, or STOP to end them.

Will Faida do things without asking me?

You decide. By default it asks before anything is sent. You can say STOP at any time.

Someone sent me a job offer. Can Faida check it?

Yes. Forward it to Faida. It checks the offer against the organisation's official channels and tells you plainly whether it is genuine. Government recruitment never asks you to pay.

What if something can't be done online?

Many offers need a paper form, a USSD code or a visit. Faida fills in the form for you to print, walks you through the code step by step, or tells you exactly where to go, when, and what to bring. Then it reminds you the day before.

Is my information safe?

Faida uses your details to find what you qualify for and to fill in your applications. An organisation sees them only when you apply to it, and only what its application asks for. Reports to organisations show totals, never names. Type “delete my data” at any time and they are deleted.

Our organisation has something to offer people. How do we list it?

Whether you are a county, a ward, a SACCO, an NGO, an employer or a college: tell us what you offer, who qualifies and when it closes, through a form or on WhatsApp. Faida verifies it and shows you who it would reach before it goes out. See Faida for organisations.
